## Step 1:

Click on the **"Sign Up"** button at the top right of the screen.

<img
  src="/public/references/create-account-1.png"
  alt="Navigating game menu from home page image"
></img>

## Step 2:

You will be redirected to the login page. Enter your email and password.

<img
  src="/public/references/create-account-2.png"
  alt="Navigating game menu from home page image"
  className="w-96"
></img>

Done! Now you have successfully created an account in Versailles.
