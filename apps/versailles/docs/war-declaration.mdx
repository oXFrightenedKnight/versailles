## What is a War Declaration?

A war declaration is a diplomatic process where nation A declares war on nation B.

## How does it work?

When nation A declares war on nation B, the game considers both nations
to be in a state of war. From then on, both nations are allowed to move their armies
onto each other's territories. Once a war between two nations is declared, all nations
receive a warning mail in their mailbox.

Please read [Army Combat](/army/combat-calculations) for more info.

## How to stop a war?

To stop a war, either of the two fighting nations must send a peace request to the other.
The peace request must be accepted by the other nation in order for the war to stop.

Please read [Peace Treaties](/diplomacy/peace-treaty) for more info.
