## What is an Available Resource?

Most buildings in Versailles produce some type of resource. This resource can belong to a **nation** or a **building**.
Nation resources are produced each turn and are automatically added to the nation's balance. Building resources, on the other hand,
are stored inside the building where they were produced and are recalculated each turn based on TBE. These resources can be exported to other
buildings to increase their TBE. Each building's resource availability can be found inside the Available Resource Component.

## What is an Available Resource Component

An **Available Resource Component** is a nested menu located inside [Building Menu](/buildings/building-menu).
Its job is to display all available **building** resources that the current building produces for export.

## How to read the Available Resource Component

All resources on the component are split into their own sections.
The section consists of the following components:

1. Resource name & icon.
2. Currently produced. Displays how much of the total possible resource the building is currently producing
   based on Total Building Efficiency (TBE). To increase the maximum produced resource capacity, upgrade the building.
3. Committed/available bar. The total bar length depends on the TBE. The bar itself is split into 2 sections: committed and free.

### Committed

Shows how much of the total produced resource is being exported via contracts.

### Free

Shows how much of the total produced resource is available for export.

4. Exact free & committed amounts. Shows the exact amounts of resources being committed and remaining free.
