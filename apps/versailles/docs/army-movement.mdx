## How to move army?

Follow instructions below to move your desired army:

### Step 1:

Start by selecting a hex that contains your nation's army on the map.

<img src="/public/references/army-movement-1.png" alt="army selection" className="w-64"></img>

### Step 2:

A dragging bar will appear at the bottom of the screen. Drag the slider to select the desired amount.

<img src="/public/references/army-movement-2.png" alt="drag-bar adjustment" className="w-64"></img>

### Step 3:

Right-click on the neighbouring tile to move your army. A read arrow will appear indicating that your army
is going to move to that tile next turn.

<img
  src="/public/references/army-movement-3.png"
  alt="right click to move army example"
  className="w-64"
></img>

## Allowed and prohibited territories

The following list describes where you **can** and **cannot** move your army. If at
least condition from the prohibited list is true, you will **not** be able to move your army.

### Can Move

1. Your own tiles.
2. Empty tiles.
3. Enemy nation tiles.

### Cannot Move

1. Any non-neighboring tile to the selected hex.
2. Any territory owned by another nation that you
   are not fighting with.
