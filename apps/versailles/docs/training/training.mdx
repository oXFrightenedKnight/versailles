## What is army training?

Army Training is a fundamental mechanic of the game. It allows players and computers
to train more units in exchange for other resources such as manpower and gold.

## How to train army?

In order to train new army, you need to [construct](/construction/construction-menu) at least one building of type **barrack**.
Each barrack has a training limit, which defines the maximum number of units this barrack can train per turn. This limit can
be expanded by further upgrading the barrack. Training speed depends on TBE and whether the current training instance exceeds
maximum training capacity of this barrack. If training capacity is exceeded, current instance training speed will be slowed down
and all consequtive instances will not gain any training progress.

In order to train new units, follow these steps:

1. Select your desired barrack and locate an [Army Training Component](/training/component).
2. Select desired army amount using **+/-** buttons.
3. Press the **"Cog"** icon the the top of the component.

Once successful, your army training request will be added to queue and begin training when all conditions are met.

## Army Cost

The standard resource pricing per unit is 1 manpower and 4 gold.
