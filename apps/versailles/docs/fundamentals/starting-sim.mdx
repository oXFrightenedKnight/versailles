## Requirements

Make sure you are logged into your account with Clerk. [How to create an account](/account/sign-up).

Make sure you have a save loaded. [How to load a save](/fundamentals/loading-saves)

## Step 1:

Start by clicking on the "Next Turn" button at the bottom of the screen.

<img src="/public/references/starting-sim-1.png" alt="Starting game sim image"></img>

In fractions of a second, your game state will update and next turn will begin. Keep repeating each
time after you made your final strategic decisions.
