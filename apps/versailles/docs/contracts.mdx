## What is a Contract?

A contract is an mutual agreement between two buildings that describes what resource
and in what amounts building A will export to building B each turn. A contact between
two buildings can be created if:

1. **Exporting** building produces at least one resource type required by **Receiving** building AND a contract
   with that resource does not already exist between these two buildings.
2. **Exporting** and **Importing** building share a valid road path.

## How it works?

All contracts go through one algorithm, that decides which contracts will or will not get executed.
If a contract is going to be unseccessful, you are going to see a red sign next to it.
All contracts are assigned an increasing order index, which is used by the algorithm to determine
which contracts will get executed first. All contracts are divided into two categories: **flexible** and **fixed**.

### Flexible

Flexible contracts are updated each turn. They are automatically calculated by the algorithm and adjusted based
on required missing amount. These contracts reduce micromanagement, but take away control.

### Fixed

The main difference from **flexible** contracts is that **fixed** contracts are never updated by the server. They work
for as long as their exported amount is not higher than their building has available. If a contract excceds building's available amount,
the contract will become **unactive** and will not be executed until this condition is met.

**Note:** Fixed contracts are always executed first.
To change contract type, toggle its ["Auto-Adjust"](/contracts/component) feature.
