## What is a Contract?

A contract is a mutual agreement between two buildings that describes which resource
and what amount Building A will export to Building B each turn. A contract between
two buildings can be created if:

1. The **Exporting** building produces at least one resource type required by the **Receiving** building AND a contract
   with that resource does not already exist between these two buildings.
2. The **Exporting** and **Importing** buildings share a valid road path.

## How does it work?

All contracts go through one algorithm that decides which contracts will or will not get executed.
If a contract is going to be unsuccessful, you are going to see a red sign next to it.
All contracts are assigned an increasing order index, which is used by the algorithm to determine
which contracts will get executed first. All contracts are divided into two categories: **flexible** and **fixed**.

### Flexible

Flexible contracts are updated each turn. They are automatically calculated by the algorithm and adjusted based
on the missing required amount. These contracts reduce micromanagement, but take away control.

### Fixed

The main difference from **flexible** contracts is that **fixed** contracts are never updated by the server. They work
for as long as their exported amount does not exceed the amount their building has available. If a contract exceeds the building's available amount,
the contract will become **inactive** and will not be executed until this condition is met.

**Note:** Fixed contracts are always executed first.
To change the contract type, toggle its ["Auto-Adjust"](/contracts/component) feature.
