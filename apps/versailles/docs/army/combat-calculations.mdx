## Combat flow:

Once you move army to a hostile nation's territory, your armies will instantly start fighting
and suffering losses. Once your army is fighting, it cannot be moved anywhere else until the battle is over.

## Suffering casualties:

Depending on whether your army is attacking or defending, it will suffer different loss percentage.
The standard loss percentage is X% of total fighting army per turn.

### Defenders

Lose a standard X% per turn.

### Attackers

Lose Y% per turn.
