---

## Requirements

Make sure you are logged into your account with Clerk. [How to create an account](/account/sign-up).

Make sure you have a save loaded. [How to load a save](/fundamentals/loading-saves)

## Step 1:

Start by selecting a hex that contains your nation's army on the map.

<img
  src="/public/references/creating-new-game-1.png"
  alt="Navigating game menu from home page image"
></img>

## Step 2:

A dragging bar will appear at the bottom of the screen. Drag the slider to select desired move amount.

<img
  src="/public/references/creating-new-game-2.png"
  alt="Navigating 'new game' button from main menu page image"
  className="w-64"
></img>

## Step 3:

Right-click on the neighbouring tile to move your army. A read arrow will appear indicating that your army
is going to move to that tile next turn.

<img
  src="/public/references/creating-new-game-2.png"
  alt="Navigating 'new game' button from main menu page image"
  className="w-64"
></img>

## Keep in mind:

You can only move army to neighbouring hexes. You are not allowed to move army to
other neutral nation's territory.
