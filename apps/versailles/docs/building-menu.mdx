## What is a Building Menu?

A building menu is a container called **"Building Configuration"**, located at the left
side panel of your game window. Each building has its own menu that consists
of different components depending on the building category and level.

## How to open a Building Menu?

To open a building menu, simply click on any hex on the map. A panel on the left side
will appear and show all available information about selected hex and its building.

## Building Menu Components

### Building Info Component

This is the standard building component that includes necessary information about current
building (ex: building level, upgrade cost, type, upgrade time, total resource produced, etc.).

### Contract Component

Please visit [Contract Component](/contracts/component).

### Available Resource Component

Please visit [Available Component](/buildings/available-component).

### Resource Consumption Component

Please visit [Resource Consumption Component](/buildings/consumption-component).

### Training Component

Army Training Component is a component that allows players to train new units
and manage existing trainings.

Please visit [Army Training](/army/training)

## How to delete a building?

To delete a building, locate the **"Trash Bin"** icon at the top of Building Info
Component.
