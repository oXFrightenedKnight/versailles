## What are Biomes?

Biomes are a fundamental attribute of every single tile in Versailles.
Biomes affect population growth and maximum population
in a tile. Biomes are drawn on every hex in all map modes.
They are also described in the [building info](/buildings/building-menu) panel on the left side.

## Types of Biomes
