## What is the Diplomacy Menu?

The Diplomacy Menu allows players to conduct diplomacy with other nations.

## How to open the Diplomacy Menu?

To open the Diplomacy Menu, locate the **Diplomacy** button on the right side of the game navbar.

<img src="/public/references/diplomacy-menu-1.png" alt="diplomacy button location"></img>

## Navigation

Once you open the Diplomacy Menu, you will see a list of all existing nations to choose
from. Select any nation you would like to manage your relations with.

The menu will be split into two sections:

### Info Section

This section is used to read information about the selected nation. The information
includes a list of ongoing wars, a list of peace treaties, the number of buildings built, the number of
occupied hexes, etc.

<img
  src="/public/references/diplomacy-info.png"
  alt="diplomacy info section"
  className="w-64"
></img>

### Action Section

This section is used to directly interact with the selected nation. Currently, you can only
declare war or send a peace request if you are fighting the selected nation.

  src="/public/references/diplomacy-action.png"
