## How to read the Military Map?

<img src="/public/references/military-map.png" alt="Military map schema"></img>

Besides the [basic layout](/map/basic-layout), the map consists of the following components:

### Army Unit Labels

Unit labels are found on top of all hexes that contain an army. The label consists
of the army owner's flag on the left and the unit count on the right.
