## How to read Military Map?

<img src="/public/references/military-map.png" alt="Military map schema"></img>

Besides the [basic layout](/map/basic-layout), the map consists of the following components:

### Army Unit Labels

Unit labels are found on top of all hexes that contain any army. The label consists
of army's owner flag on the left and unit amount on the right.
