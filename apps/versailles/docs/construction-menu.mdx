## What is Construction Menu?

Construction Menu is used to construct and upgrade buildings and manage existing constructions.

## How to open Construction Menu?

To open Construction Menu, locate the **"Build"** button on the right side of the game navbar.

<img src="/public/references/construction-menu-1.png" alt="Construction menu on the screen"></img>

## How to construct a new building?

To construct a new building, follow these steps:

1. Open Construction Menu.
2. At the top section of the menu, click on the icon of a building that you want to construct.
3. Click on hex/hexes that you want to build this building on. Ensure you have enough gold saved.

Once a building is queued, it will begin constructing. Construction time depends
on building type and level.

When construction is finished, a building will appear on the chosen tile.

## How to upgrade an existing building?

To upgrade an existing building, follow all the instructions above. **Note:**
make sure your chosen construction category matches the existing building.

## How to construct a road?

Follow these steps to construct a new road:

1. Open Construction Menu.
2. Select **"Road"** icon from the top mode selection.
3. Click on a hex you would like your road to begin at.
4. Once selected, move your cursor following a path you want your road to have.
5. Once you have made a path to your destination tile, simply click on it.

**Note:** your new road road **can** intersect another road, but it cannot share
segments with existing roads.

## How to cancel existing construction?

To cancel existing construction, open Construction Menu and click on the **"X"** icon
of the desired construction instance you want to cancel.
