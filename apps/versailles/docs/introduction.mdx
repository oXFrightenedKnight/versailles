## What is Versailles?

Versailles is an open-source browser game. It features war simulation, diplomacy, economy management, and advanced AI.
It was made by a solo developer as a non-commercial pet project.

## How it works

The game is server-authoritative, which means all requests are sent to and processed on the server. The browser simply renders
and applies optimistic updates by tracking player actions and merging them with server data.

## What you will learn in this guide

This guide is intended for people who just found out about Versailles and want to get better at Versailles and learn
new tricks and strategies.
