---
title: "Starting a new game"
description: "In this section, you will learn how to start a new game in Versailles."
---

## Requirements

Make sure you are logged into your account with Clerk. [How to create an account](/account/sign-up).

## Step 1:

Start by clicking on the "Menu" tab at the top of the Home page or glowing rectangle
with the play button on it at the bottom.

<img
  src="/public/references/creating-new-game-1.png"
  alt="Navigating game menu from home page image"
></img>

## Step 2:

Once the main menu has loaded, click "New Game".

<img
  src="/public/references/creating-new-game-2.png"
  alt="Navigating 'new game' button from main menu page image"
  className="w-64"
></img>

The game will load and automatically launch after a couple of seconds.
