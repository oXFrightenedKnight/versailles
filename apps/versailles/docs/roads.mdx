## What are roads?

Roads are a fundamental part of your nation's economy. Without them, your buildings
cannot transfer resources. Road paths are shows with white, dashed lines and **can**
be used in any direction.

<img src="/public/references/road-example.png" alt="road path example"></img>

## How should they be placed?

In order for buildings to successfully exchange resources, they must have at least one valid,
continious path **including** both hexes of **exporting** and **receiving** building

## How much does road construction cost?

The standard price per single road segment is 50 Gold. Depending on your path length,
you may have varying totals.

Please visit [Construction](/construction/construction-menu) for more info on road construction.
