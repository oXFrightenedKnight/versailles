## Basic Actions

Navigating the map consists of the following actions:

### Panning

Hold the left mouse button and drag to pan the map.

### Zooming

Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in/out.

## Switching Between Map Modes

To switch between map modes, simply click on **"Build"** for
[Economy Map](/map/economy) or **"Diplomacy"** for [Military Map](/map/military)
on the right side of the game navbar.
