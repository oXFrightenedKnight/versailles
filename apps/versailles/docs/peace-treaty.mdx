## What is a Peace Treaty?

A peace treaty is an agreement signed by two fighting nations to stop fighting and
remove war state.

## How does it work?

When two nations are [at war](/diplomacy/war-declaration), one of them can send a **peace request** to another.
The peace request goes into nation's mailbox and expires in 3 turns. If reciever nation rejects
the offer, the war will continue. In case of approval, both nations will automatically sign a
**peace treaty**. A peace treaty usually lasts 30 turns, and during that time the two
nations cannot declare war or attack each other.
