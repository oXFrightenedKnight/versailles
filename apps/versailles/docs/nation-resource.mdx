## What is a Nation Resource?

<img src="/public/references/nation-resources.png" alt="nation resources example"></img>

A Nation Resource is a resource that is linked to a specific nation. Nation Resources
are used for army training and construction. Nation Resources are always spent by
the player and have no storage limit.
