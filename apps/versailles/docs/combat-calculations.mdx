## Combat flow:

Once you move an army to a hostile nation's territory, your armies will instantly start fighting
and suffering losses. Once your army is fighting, it cannot be moved anywhere else until the battle is over.

## Suffering casualties:

Depending on whether your army is attacking or defending, it will suffer a different loss percentage.
The exact loss amount depends on the relative strength of attackers and defenders.

### Defenders

Take 12% of attacker's total strength.

### Attackers

Take 15% of defender's total strength.

**Note**: The minimum number of casualties per turn is 1.
